How to Use Your Logbook

Detailed visual walkthroughs for everyday firearm logging tasks.

1. Logging a New Firearm Acquisition

Pursuant to 27 CFR § 478.125(f), record firearms within 7 days of receipt.

STEP 1

Click "+ New Acquisition"

Click the amber button at the top right of your screen or press Ctrl + K / Cmd + K.

STEP 2

Use C&R Autocomplete

Type the Manufacturer or Model. Click the autocomplete dropdown to auto-fill official C&R specifications and section citations.

STEP 3

Save Entry

Enter the Serial Number, Caliber, Acquisition Date, and Seller/Transferor Name and click "Save Acquisition Record".

2. Transferring or Disposing a Firearm

When selling or transferring a C&R firearm to another collector or FFL dealer.

STEP 1

Select the Firearm

Click on the firearm row in your Bound Book table to open the right-hand Inspection Drawer.

STEP 2

Click "Log Disposition"

Click the purple "Log Disposition" button at the bottom of the inspection panel.

STEP 3

Enter Buyer Details

Enter the Buyer's Name, Address, FFL Number (if applicable), and Transfer Date. The record will be permanently locked against non-audit edits.

3. Printing Bound Book for Compliance Audit

Generate a clean, official PDF matching ATF Ruling 2016-1 table formats.

1. Click the "Print PDF" button in the top action header bar.

2. Enter your Licensee/Collector Name and FFL License Number.

3. Click "Download Printable PDF". Your operating system's native save dialog will open allowing you to choose where to save your PDF (or print directly).

Encrypted Backup Vault & Seed Phrase Guide

Protect your collection records against computer hardware failures.

BIP-39 AES-256-GCM Vault

What is a 12-Word Seed Phrase?

Think of your 12-word seed phrase like a digital combination lock for a safe. When you generate a backup, the application creates a 12-word phrase (e.g. "alpha bravo charlie delta...").

IMPORTANT SAFEGUARD:

Write your 12 words down on a piece of paper and keep them in a safe place. Anyone with your 12 words can restore your backup vault file.

How to Create an Encrypted Backup (`.crbk`)

  1. Click "Vault (.crbk)" in the top action bar.
  2. Click "Generate New 12-Word Seed Phrase".
  3. Write down the 12 words in exact order on paper.
  4. Click "Download Encrypted Archive" and save your `.crbk` backup file to a USB flash drive or external drive.
