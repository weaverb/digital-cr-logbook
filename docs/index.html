A digital bound book for Curio & Relic collectors

This application keeps your Type 03 FFL acquisition and disposition records, plus maintenance and range logs and an offline copy of the ATF's C&R reference list, on your own computer. It's free, it works without an internet connection, and there's no account to create.

Already licensed? Jump to installation or how to use it. Still working toward a Type 03 license? Start with getting licensed below.

Built with real effort to follow 27 CFR § 478.125(f) and ATF Ruling 2016-1, but this is an independent open-source project, not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by the U.S. Government or the ATF. Nothing here is legal advice.

New to C&R collecting?

This tool is built around the FFL Type 03 — Collector of Curios and Relics license. It lets an individual collector acquire and hold firearms the ATF has designated as Curios or Relics — generally older, historically or collector-significant arms — without going through a dealer transfer for each one, and this logbook is the bound book you're required to keep once you have it.

A full step-by-step guide to applying for a Type 03 FFL is on the way and will live here once it's ready. In the meantime, the ATF's own page on Curios & Relics is the authoritative place to start:

How to use it

The core bound-book workflow, in four parts.

1. Logging a new acquisition

27 CFR § 478.125(f) requires recording a firearm within 7 days of receipt.

  1. Step 1

    Click "New Acquisition", or press Ctrl/Cmd + K.

  2. Step 2

    Type the manufacturer or model and pick it from the C&R autocomplete to fill in the official classification.

  3. Step 3

    Enter the serial number, caliber, acquisition date, and seller/transferor, then save.

2. Transferring or disposing of a firearm

When you sell or transfer a C&R firearm to another collector or dealer.

  1. Step 1

    Click the firearm's row in your bound book to open its detail panel.

  2. Step 2

    Click "Log Disposition" at the bottom of the panel.

  3. Step 3

    Enter the buyer's name, address, FFL number (if any), and the transfer date. The record is then permanently locked against non-audit edits.

3. Printing your bound book for an inspection

Generates a PDF formatted to match ATF Ruling 2016-1 table layouts.

  1. Step 1

    Click "Print PDF" in the top action bar.

  2. Step 2

    Enter your licensee name and FFL number for the header.

  3. Step 3

    Click "Download Printable PDF" and choose where to save it.

4. Updating the C&R reference library

Search 4,200+ pre-loaded classifications, or drop in a newer ATF-published list without waiting for an app update.

  1. Step 1

    Open the "ATF Master C&R Reference Library" tab.

  2. Step 2

    Click "Import Updated CSV" and drop in an updated master list file.

  3. Step 3

    Click "Apply & Replace C&R List". The library and autocomplete update immediately — you can revert to the default list any time.

Other features

Not required by federal statute, but useful for tracking the rest of a collection's history.

Maintenance log

Cleaning history, cosmoline removal, parts replacement, and gunsmith work, tracked per firearm with cost and notes.

Tab: "Maintenance Log"

Range & ammunition log

Round counts, ammo brand and grain weight, group sizes, and total rounds fired — a way to keep an eye on barrel wear over time.

Tab: "Range & Ammo Log"

FFL contacts & photos

An address book for dealers, collectors, and auction houses you work with, plus a place to attach photos of proof marks, crests, and cartouches — stored locally.

Tabs: "FFL Contacts" & "Media Gallery"

Backing up your records

Everything is stored on your own device, so backups are on you.

What's a 12-word seed phrase?

When you create a backup, the app generates a 12-word phrase that encrypts it — think of it as the combination to a lock. You'll need the exact same 12 words, in order, to restore that backup later.

Write the 12 words down on paper and keep them somewhere safe. Anyone with those words can open your backup file.

Creating a backup

  1. Click "Vault (.crbk)" in the top action bar.
  2. Click "Generate New 12-Word Seed Phrase".
  3. Write the 12 words down, in order.
  4. Click "Download Encrypted Archive" and save the file somewhere other than this computer — a USB drive works well.
